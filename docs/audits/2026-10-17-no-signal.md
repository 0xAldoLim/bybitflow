## Verdict

reversals from reaching their mandatory price-action confirmation. Other
candidates failed valid strategy gates. Discord did not drop a qualified signal
during this process: zero candidates reached final confirmation and no new
initial webhook requests were attempted.

## Reproduced defect

Stressed short intraday reversals require two completed five-minute reclaim/hold
bars **after** the structural trigger becomes available. Generation instead set
their trigger deadline to two minutes after discovery. For discoveries shortly
after the trigger, both required bars could not close before expiry.

Of 97 stressed short intraday reversals, 58 were already structurally invalid.
Among the remaining 39 evaluated candidates, 37 had entry deadlines earlier
than the earliest possible close of the two required bars. Six evaluated
candidates had supportive structure, flow, participation and factor assessments
but lacked reclaim/hold confirmation. Some also failed independent profile,
entry or risk checks; this is not evidence that 37 alerts should have been sent.

For example, AEROUSDT event `954b39f5c9d29ff4549fd84d` had a trigger available
at 18:15, an entry deadline at 18:17:09, and mandatory bars that could first
complete at 18:25. Its other risk and mechanism failures remain valid exclusions.

## Repair

For newly generated plans, schedule the same two-minute entry opportunity after
the required bars can first close, capped by the original setup expiry. Actual
reclaim/hold, fresh executed flow, participation, profile, entry quality and risk
checks remain mandatory. A scheduled timestamp does not establish confirmation.

Normal short intraday, continuation, core intraday and swing timing retain their
existing entry duration. Generation does not rewrite saved plans, reopen expired
events, extend active setup lifecycles, alter historical snapshots, retrain ML,
change scoring or thresholds, or manufacture Discord messages.

The configured minimum net reward-to-risk remains 2.0. A QNT short with current
net reward-to-risk of 1.90 was correctly rejected; an SEI long was excluded by
opposing absorption despite sufficient reward-to-risk. These are separate from
the scheduling defect.

## Regression coverage

`tests/test_confirmation_timing.py` covers both directions, unavailable future
bars, weak flow, insufficient profile confidence, unchanged timing for unaffected
families, setup-expiry bounds, late discovery and generation without rearming
persisted setups. Existing scanner-to-notifier tests also verify successful
deterministic qualification and delivery without an ML champion.

This repair makes the affected confirmation path reachable. It does not establish
that a qualifying market setup exists or promise an alert within a fixed period.
