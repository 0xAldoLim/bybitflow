## Review scope and limitations

Every unique file received a structural/relevance assessment. PDFs were extracted locally, with OCR for 3,947 initially text-sparse pages and rotated OCR recovery for sideways Camerer/Stock Patterns scans. The review examined titles, structure, relevant passages and implementation applicability across the library; it is **not a claim of line-by-line reading of all 31,169 pages or verification of every mathematical derivation**. Text extraction and keyword indexes alone are not semantic review. The research ledger cites passages actually inspected for implementation decisions.

All 122 Field of Vision course slides and the stationarity PNG were visually inspected. The complete 87-minute DVD3 local machine transcript was reviewed alongside its corresponding slides; caption errors and unverified personal/historical anecdotes are not implementation evidence. A TXT-linked external lecture was not readable beyond its page title and was not used. Chart-only/blank pages may have little OCR text; that is not treated as proof that the underlying chart was understood. No entry is marked as having an exhaustive page-by-page book review.

Readability below means local content could be accessed, not that a source's strategy was validated. Duplicate entries inherit their canonical review and are explicitly identified. Source confidence describes provenance/assumption limits, never a trading probability.
