# BybitFlow engineering contract

- BybitFlow researches public perpetual markets and delivers alerts. It never places orders or claims exchange fills, account profit, or a validated win rate from a research score.
- Inspect the actual branch, remote revision, working tree and deployed commit before changes. Treat repository implementation, tests and durable runtime evidence as sources of truth for their own scopes. Preserve local configuration, secrets and unrelated files. Investigate measured causes before making operational changes.
- Preserve published plans, lifecycle and historical policy semantics. Changes to market qualification, scoring, costs, targets, stops, timing or risk require explicit scope and independent evidence. A lack of alerts is not a reason to weaken evidence or risk gates. Never impose a daily quota or top-N cap on distinct qualified setups.
- Preserve source, receipt and event times, schema and policy provenance, frozen decisions and unknown states. Quotes, prints, candle proxies and reported fills have different evidentiary strength. Missing, corrupt, stale or ambiguous evidence cannot become a verified outcome. Do not rewrite decisions or completed labels to suit a later policy.
- Keep ML eligibility source- and schema-specific. Preserve chronological splits, purge, embargo, unseen holdouts, verified-cost requirements and fail-closed compatibility checks. Fitting is not approval. Proxy tracks stay advisory. Production promotion requires a named review and every registry gate.
- Keep forward research independent of delivery and production ML. Freeze causal baselines, costs, admission, comparisons and evaluation windows before observation. Report uncertainty, dependence and execution assumptions. An outcome never authorizes an earlier decision.
- Use one scanner, recorder, lifecycle and delivery pipeline with bounded background work. Prioritize critical recording and existing lifecycle monitoring over discovery, reports, recovery and fitting. Keep existing venue subscriptions where safe. Distinguish storage dependency, backpressure, SQLite contention, clock uncertainty, network failure and evidence gaps. Retry transient failures without warming up on fabricated data.
- Use existing retention classifications, range leases, checkpoints and durable cleanup audits. Protect active plans, unresolved outcomes, replay readers and permanent evidence. Apply deletion only to eligible managed raw files. Failed verification may be reconciled only with sufficient frozen pre-run evidence. Preserve failure and recovery history. Missing audits, changed frozen rows or artifacts, and unverifiable evidence block cleanup. Never raise capacity or truncate history to conceal a failure.
- Use short SQLite transactions and bounded, indexed diagnostics. Preserve WAL and migration receipts. Use repository backup and restore procedures. Do not manually delete SQLite files or force live checkpoint, vacuum or migration bypass. Keep worker status and storage measurements timestamped.
- A failed broad cleanup stays blocked without its original frozen proof. Independent targeted raw retirement requires its own durable current pre- and post-operation proof, a fresh check that each raw target has no committed consumer, and preservation of frozen ML data, active plans and Discord receipts. It must never claim to reconcile the historical broad failure. Preserve durable incomplete outcomes while retiring their proven unusable raw bytes.
- Discord lifecycle updates require the exact setup's durable, successful initial receipt for its original destination. Related setups remain independently monitored. Preserve duplicate suppression, outage episode deduplication, terminal ordering and uncertain-delivery safeguards. Use read-only webhook checks unless test delivery is explicitly authorized.
- Deploy the exact pushed revision after all required Python, container and security CI checks pass. Use the existing targeted deployment workflow, preserve `.env` and the research-data volume, run migrations normally and compare pre/post active plans, receipts, models and durable evidence. A failed health gate requires a precise report and investigation. Do not force deployment, restart the host or replace data to obtain a green status.
- Add focused regression coverage for correctness changes, run normal verification, and exercise relevant existing entry points. Label observed behavior and proposed behavior accurately. Write public documentation for any clone location and keep current incident values and private diagnostics out of the repository.
